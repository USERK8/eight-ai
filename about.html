about the project

eight ai

  • lightweight & capable: a fine-tuned 1B parameter language model built for speed and utility.
  • on-device native: heavily optimized for snapdragon-powered androids—bringing real AI locally.
  • under the hood: configured with 4-bit quantization and a 32K vocabulary for peak efficiency.
  • the stack: hosted seamlessly via hugging face spaces and routed through cloudflare workers.
